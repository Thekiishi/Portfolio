Programme Overview

PAID – Roots and Roofs is an EU-funded cultural heritage initiative delivered under the EUNIC Spaces of Culture framework, piloted across Nigeria's six geopolitical zones. The programme focuses on preserving pan-African indigenous skills in interior and exterior architecture — traditional building methods, materials, and spatial practices at risk of being lost as urbanisation accelerates.

I joined as Programme Communications Coordinator on a fixed-term contract from January 1 to February 17, 2026. My remit covered the full communications and coordination layer: managing the facilitator pipeline, onboarding participants, maintaining stakeholder communications across 7 partner organisations, and executing the programme's social media strategy.

The programme was delivered on schedule with 15 facilitators confirmed across all six zones, 24 participants onboarded, and comprehensive handover documentation produced at contract conclusion to ensure continuity beyond my engagement.

My Contributions

Facilitator Pipeline Management

Managed the full facilitator journey post-acceptance: onboarding communications, session scheduling, contract and invoice processing in line with Goethe-Institut financial requirements, travel logistics coordination, and document compliance tracking. Built and maintained a facilitator tracking system across all 15 confirmed facilitators spanning six zones.

Participant & Stakeholder Communications

Designed and maintained stakeholder-specific communication streams for each group: facilitators, participants, jury members, and partner organisations. Built a participant contact database organised by geopolitical zone and developed a full email template library covering all recurring communication touchpoints.

Coordination Infrastructure

Built the programme's coordination infrastructure independently — facilitator tracking system, participant database, and communications template library. Produced status reports for programme leadership and ensured all documentation was maintained to Goethe-Institut standards throughout.

Social Media Strategy & Execution

Developed and executed the programme's social media strategy for the @paid_rootsandroofs account, including content planning, live workshop coverage, and stakeholder tagging strategy across partner institutions.

Handover Documentation

Created comprehensive handover documentation upon contract conclusion covering all active workstreams, contact databases, tracking systems, and outstanding items — ensuring zero information loss and full programme continuity beyond my engagement.

06

Reflections

"Managing communications across seven institutional partners simultaneously while maintaining facilitator and participant pipelines taught me that coordination infrastructure isn't support work — it is the work."

The most demanding aspect of this role was maintaining quality and consistency across multiple parallel communication streams, each with different stakeholders, different tones, and different information needs. Building the template library early in the contract was the single decision that made everything else manageable.

Working within the Goethe-Institut's financial and administrative standards was a valuable experience in institutional programme management — understanding that compliance and documentation are not bureaucratic overhead but the foundation that makes cross-institutional collaboration possible at this scale.

The handover documentation I produced at contract conclusion reflected a commitment I hold to every engagement: the work should outlast the contract. A programme should never lose momentum because one person's involvement ends.
